Why Competitors Rank Higher

The primary reasons competitors outrank Zyphuel are not content quality (yours is excellent) but infrastructure and authority signals:

  1. Custom Domain (Priority 1): zyphuel.netlify.app is a subdomain. Google gives significantly less domain authority to subdomains of free hosting platforms. A zyphuel.com or zyphuel.pk domain would immediately boost trust signals. This is the single biggest SEO blocker.
  2. Backlink Profile (Priority 2): Zero or very few external backlinks means zero domain authority in Google's eyes. Submit to Pakistan business directories (Jaxy.pk, PakBiz, Starter.pk), claim Google Business Profile, pursue local press coverage and partnerships.
  3. App Store Listing (Priority 3): Even a basic Google Play Store listing creates a verified entity signal that Google weights heavily for brand queries. Competitors have this, Zyphuel doesn't.
  4. Urdu Content (Priority 4): No competitor has bilingual content. Urdu pages targeting "پٹرول ڈلیوری لاہور" would capture a massive untapped keyword space with zero competition.
  5. Consistent Publishing (Priority 5): Aim for 2+ articles per month covering seasonal fuel topics, OGRA rate changes, and local Lahore energy news. The 3 new November articles help, but consistency signals freshness to crawlers.
