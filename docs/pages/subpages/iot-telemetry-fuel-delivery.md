## Article Synopsis & Core Concepts
- Deep-dive technical exploration into the mechanics of retail petrol station short-fueling in Pakistan (where calibration drift or tampering causes 5% to 12% loss to motorists).
- Technical dissection of Zyphuel’s positive-displacement flow meters equipped with high-resolution optical pulse encoders measuring volume down to 0.01 litres.
- Explains automatic temperature compensation (ATC) calibrated to the 15°C petroleum reference standard to eliminate thermal expansion volumetric loss during Lahore’s intense summer heat.
- Details the encrypted Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) link that streams millisecond flow metrics from the truck nozzle directly to the user’s mobile application screen in real time.
- Discusses tamper-proof digital receipts tied directly to GPS coordinates and meter serial numbers.
