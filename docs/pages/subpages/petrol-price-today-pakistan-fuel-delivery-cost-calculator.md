- Under OGRA’s daily fuel pricing mechanism, ex-depot rates are calculated using a rolling 7-day international Platts Arab Gulf index with locked weekend rates.
- Zyphuel applies a transparent retail pump margin markup of +Rs. 5.00/L over ex-depot rates, matching compliant forecourt pricing.

- The transparent pricing formula is: Total Cost = (Fuel Volume in Litres × [Official Ex-Depot Rate + Rs. 5.00]) + Delivery Fee.
